# The Hows of Us

## About the Movie
**The Hows of Us** is a 2018 Filipino romantic drama film directed by *Cathy Garcia-Molina* and starring  
*Kathryn Bernardo* and *Daniel Padilla*.  
It tells the story of a young couple who struggle to keep their relationship alive while chasing their dreams.
